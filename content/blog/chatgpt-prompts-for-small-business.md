ChatGPT can save a small business owner hours a week, but only if you know what to ask. Vague prompts get vague answers. This guide gives you 40 copy-and-paste prompts organized by task, plus the simple formula that makes any prompt work better. Replace anything in [brackets] with your details.

Want better results from any prompt? See [prompt engineering](/glossary/prompt-engineering) for what makes a prompt work, and [fine-tuning](/glossary/fine-tuning) for when a custom-trained model is (rarely) worth it.

## How do you write a good ChatGPT prompt for a small business?

**A good prompt gives ChatGPT a role, context about your business, a specific task and the format you want back.** The more real detail you include, the less editing you'll do.

Use this formula:

> **You are** [role]. **My business** is [what you do, where, who you serve]. **Write/Create** [the task]. **Format:** [length, tone, structure].

Example:

> You are the office manager for a family-owned HVAC company in Phoenix that serves homeowners. Write a friendly text message reminding a customer about their AC tune-up tomorrow between 8 and 10am. Keep it under 300 characters and include an option to reschedule.

Four rules that make a big difference:

1. **Be specific.** "Write a follow-up email" is weak. "Write a 3-sentence follow-up to a homeowner who got a $6,000 roof quote last week" is strong.
2. **Give it your voice.** Paste a message you've written before and say "match this tone."
3. **Ask for options.** "Give me 3 versions" lets you pick the best one.
4. **Iterate.** Reply "shorter," "more casual" or "add a deadline" instead of starting over.

## Prompts for customer communication

1. "Write a friendly reply to a customer asking [question]. My business is [details]. Keep it under 100 words."
2. "Rewrite this message so it sounds professional but warm: [paste message]."
3. "Write a text confirming a customer's appointment on [date] at [time], with a link to reschedule: [link]."
4. "Write an apology email to a customer whose job was delayed because [reason]. Offer [what you'll do to make it right]."
5. "Write a polite payment reminder for an invoice that's [number] days overdue. Make it firm but friendly."
6. "Turn these job notes into a clear summary for the customer: [paste notes]."
7. "Write 5 answers to the most common questions customers ask a [type of business], in plain English."
8. "Translate this message into Spanish, keeping the tone friendly and professional: [message]."

## Prompts for sales and quotes

9. "Write a follow-up text for a customer who received a quote for [service] [number] days ago and hasn't replied."
10. "Write a short, clear quote description for [job], explaining what's included and what isn't."
11. "List 10 objections customers have about [service] and a helpful, honest response to each."
12. "Write a 3-message follow-up sequence for leads who requested a quote but haven't booked."
13. "Write a referral request message for happy customers that offers [incentive, if any]."
14. "Create a phone script for answering a new customer call about [service], including questions to qualify the job."

## Prompts for reviews and reputation

15. "Write a short text asking a happy customer to leave a Google review, with this link: [link]."
16. "Write a professional reply to this positive review: [paste review]."
17. "Write a calm, helpful reply to this negative review that takes responsibility and offers to fix it offline: [paste review]." See our full guide on [how to respond to negative reviews](/blog/how-to-respond-to-negative-reviews).
18. "Summarize the common themes in these customer reviews and suggest 3 improvements: [paste reviews]."

## Prompts for marketing

19. "Write 5 Google Business Profile posts for a [type of business] in [city] for [month]."
20. "Write a 150-word 'About Us' section for my business: [details about history, team, values]."
21. "Write a service page outline for [service] in [city], including the questions customers ask most."
22. "Give me 20 blog post ideas a [type of business] customer would actually search for."
23. "Write 3 Facebook posts promoting [offer], each under 80 words, with a clear call to action."
24. "Write a seasonal email to past customers reminding them about [seasonal service]."
25. "Write 10 short headlines for a Google ad for [service] in [city], each under 30 characters."
26. "Rewrite this website section to be clearer and more persuasive for homeowners: [paste text]."

## Prompts for hiring and team

27. "Write a job post for a [role] at a [type of business] in [city]. Include pay range [range], schedule and requirements."
28. "Create 10 interview questions for a [role] that reveal reliability and customer service skills."
29. "Write a friendly onboarding checklist for a new [role] for their first week."
30. "Write a short, clear policy for [topic, e.g., using company vehicles], in plain language."

## Prompts for operations

31. "Turn these steps into a clear standard operating procedure with numbered steps: [paste steps]." Learn more about [SOPs](/glossary/sop).
32. "Create a checklist for [task, e.g., finishing a bathroom remodel] so nothing gets missed."
33. "Summarize this long email thread into the key decisions and next steps: [paste thread]."
34. "Help me plan next week's schedule for [number] technicians given these jobs: [list]."
35. "List the most time-consuming admin tasks in a [type of business] and how each could be automated."

## Prompts for finance and planning

36. "Explain this invoice or bill in plain English and flag anything unusual: [paste text without account numbers]."
37. "Help me estimate the break-even point if my monthly costs are [amount] and my average job profit is [amount]."
38. "Create a simple monthly budget template for a [type of business] with [number] employees."
39. "Compare the pros and cons of hiring a part-time admin versus automating [tasks]." See [AI admin assistant vs. hiring](/blog/ai-admin-assistant-vs-hiring).
40. "Give me 10 questions to ask my accountant before tax season."

## What should you never put into ChatGPT?

**Never paste passwords, bank or card numbers, Social Security numbers, health information, or confidential customer and contract details into a chatbot.** Check your plan's data and privacy settings, and keep sensitive work in tools designed for it.
