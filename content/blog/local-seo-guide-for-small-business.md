When someone searches "electrician near me" or "best dentist in Denver," Google shows a map with three businesses and a list below it. Local SEO is how you earn a spot there. This beginner's guide explains how it works and what to do first.

## What is local SEO?

**Local SEO is the work of improving how your business appears when nearby customers search on Google and Google Maps.** It matters most for businesses that serve a specific area, like trades, clinics, restaurants and local shops.

## How does Google rank local businesses?

**Google ranks local results mainly on relevance, distance and prominence.**

| Factor | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance | How well your business matches the search | Right categories, services, descriptions and website content |
| Distance | How close you are to the searcher or searched location | Accurate address or service area |
| Prominence | How well known and trusted you are | Reviews, links, mentions and an active presence |

## Step 1: Set up your Google Business Profile

**Your Google Business Profile is the foundation of local SEO.** Claim it, verify it and fill out every section: categories, services, hours, photos and description. Follow our full [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-checklist).

## Step 2: Get steady reviews

**Reviews are one of the strongest local signals and the first thing customers check.** Ask every happy customer, every time, with a direct link. Respond to every review. See [how to get more Google reviews automatically](/blog/get-more-google-reviews-automatically) and [how to respond to negative reviews](/blog/how-to-respond-to-negative-reviews).

## Step 3: Keep your business details consistent

**Your name, address and phone number (NAP) should be identical everywhere online.** Check directories like Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Facebook and industry sites. Learn more about [NAP consistency](/glossary/nap-consistency) and [local citations](/glossary/local-seo).

## Step 4: Build a website Google can understand

**Your website should clearly explain what you do, where you do it and how to contact you, and load fast on phones.**

- A page for each main service.
- Your service area clearly listed.
- Click-to-call buttons and a simple contact form.
- Structured data that tells Google your business details. See [schema markup](/glossary/schema-markup).
- Fast load times and a mobile-friendly design.

## Step 5: Create helpful location pages (carefully)

**If you serve several cities, create location pages only when you can make each one genuinely useful.** Google treats near-identical pages with the city name swapped as doorway pages, which can hurt your site. Good location pages include real local details: the services you offer there, local projects, reviews from customers in that area and answers to local questions.

## Step 6: Earn local links and mentions

**Links and mentions from local organizations, news sites, suppliers and community groups build prominence.**

- Sponsor a local team or event.
- Join your chamber of commerce and trade associations.
- Get listed with suppliers and partners.
- Share expertise with local news.

## Step 7: Track your results

**Track calls, direction requests, website clicks and rankings each month.** Use your Google Business Profile performance data, Google Search Console and call tracking. See [call tracking](/glossary/call-tracking).
