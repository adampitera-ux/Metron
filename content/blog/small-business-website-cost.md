"How much does a website cost?" is like asking how much a truck costs. It depends on what you need it to do. This guide breaks down the main options, what's included, the costs people forget, and how to decide what's worth paying for.

## How much does a small business website cost?

**A small business website can cost anything from a low monthly DIY subscription to several thousand dollars or more for a custom agency build, plus ongoing hosting and maintenance.** The right budget depends on how much you'll do yourself and how much the site needs to bring in business.

| Option | Typical pricing model | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| DIY website builder | Monthly subscription | Low upfront cost; full control | Your time; design limits; you handle SEO and updates |
| Freelancer | One-time project fee, sometimes plus hosting | Custom design at a moderate price | Quality varies; maintenance may not be included |
| Agency | Larger project fee, sometimes a retainer | Strategy, design and SEO expertise | Highest upfront cost |
| Managed website plan | Setup fee + monthly fee | Built, hosted and maintained for you; predictable cost | Ongoing monthly commitment |

## What's included in the cost of a website?

**Website pricing usually covers some mix of design, building pages, writing content, setting up hosting and making the site work on phones.** Always ask what's included in writing:

- Number of pages.
- Custom design or a template.
- Who writes the content.
- Mobile-friendly design.
- Contact forms and click-to-call buttons.
- Basic SEO setup: page titles, descriptions, structure and speed.
- Hosting, SSL and backups.
- How many revisions are included.

## What ongoing costs should you budget for?

**Budget for the domain, hosting, security, maintenance and content updates every year.**

- **Domain name:** renewed yearly.
- **Hosting:** where your site lives online.
- **SSL and security:** the padlock in the browser, plus protection from attacks.
- **Updates and backups:** keeping software current and recoverable.
- **Content changes:** new services, prices, photos and team members.
- **Your time:** if you maintain it yourself, your hours count.

## What hidden costs catch business owners off guard?

**The most common surprises are content writing, stock photos, extra revisions, plugins and paying someone to fix things later.**

- Being asked to write all the content yourself.
- Paying extra for each revision round.
- Premium plugins or apps with their own subscriptions.
- Fixing a slow or broken site after launch.
- Rebuilding the site because it can't be found on Google.

## How do you decide what's worth paying for?

**Pay for the things that turn visitors into customers: speed, clear messaging, easy contact and being findable on Google.** A cheap site that nobody finds or that doesn't get calls is the most expensive option.

Ask any provider:

1. Will the site load quickly on a phone?
2. Will it clearly show my services and service area?
3. Will customers be able to call or request a quote in one tap?
4. Is basic SEO included?
5. Who updates it after launch, and what does that cost?
6. Do I own my domain and content?
