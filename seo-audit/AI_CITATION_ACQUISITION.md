# AI Citation & Authority Acquisition

**Principle:** answer engines cite sources they already trust (directories, publications, communities, reviews). There is no shortcut and no fabrication.

## Observed citation landscape (2026-10-12 SERPs)
| Source type | Example seen | Relevance | Recommended approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agency directories | [DesignRush](https://www.designrush.com) AI companies list, [Clutch](https://clutch.co), [G2](https://www.g2.com) (ResultantAI profile seen), [Techreviewer](https://techreviewer.co/ai-automation-agencies) | High: appear on "best agency" SERPs | Create honest profiles; earn reviews from real clients |
| Google Maps pack | 3 AI agencies with reviews | High for "near me" style queries | Google Business Profile only if eligible |
| Reddit communities | r/agency, r/automation, r/AI_Agents, r/smallbusiness | High: Google ranks these threads | Ethan answers questions genuinely, with disclosure; no astroturfing |
| Forbes / business media | Forbes small-business AI agents article | Medium | Pitch original data later (see assets) |
| Trade associations | Local chambers, ACCA (HVAC), NRCA (roofing), NPMA (pest) | Medium-High for industry trust | Membership listings; offer educational talks |
| Software partner directories | Cal.com, CRM marketplaces | Medium | Only if a real partnership or certification exists |

Contacts were **not** compiled; verify each before outreach.

## Outreach policy
Honest value-based outreach only. No paid links, PBNs, mass guest posts, fake reviews, fake quotes or fake forum accounts.

**Measure:** referring domains (GSC Links report), referral sessions, branded search impressions, re-test prompts P01/P16.
