**Method:** live Google searches in Chrome (hl=en, gl=us, logged-in profile, so possibly personalized) for 3 queries, plus web-search tool results for 6 more queries. No traffic, ranking or backlink metrics are claimed; none were available.

- [Trillet](https://trillet.ai)
- [Sophiie](https://www.sophiie.ai)
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io)

**Pattern:** product companies publish "N best AI receptionists for HVAC (2026)" comparison posts that rank their own product first, plus per-industry feature pages. Several call out emergency keywords ("no heat", "gas smell") and named field-service-software integrations.
**Metron opportunity:** Metron is a done-for-you service, not a SaaS product. Its angle is "we set up and manage the receptionist, follow-up, reviews and website together for a flat fee." Don't compete on the head term; compete on bundled, managed outcomes and the industry long-tail.
