## Content brief template (apply per priority industry)
1. **Who:** the owner or office manager of a 2–30 person company.
2. **Problems:** missed calls during jobs, slow estimate follow-up, manual scheduling and paperwork.
3. **Workflows to automate:** answer and triage calls, text back missed calls, run an estimate follow-up sequence, send review requests after the job, send invoice reminders.
4. **How it works:** step-by-step from first call to booked job.
5. **Software:** list as "commonly connected"; integration specifics require confirmation per client. **Never claim a certified integration.**
6. **Human approval:** pricing exceptions, insurance disputes, refunds and emergencies.
7. **Implementation requirements:** phone forwarding, CRM access, approved price list and FAQs.
8. **Benefits:** describe the mechanism; never a guaranteed percentage.
9. **Pricing:** link to /pricing (Scale plan).
10. **FAQs:** from real buyer questions (People Also Ask and Reddit threads).
11. **CTA:** free audit or a call with Ethan.
